AvaStars Collector - Chrome extension for AvaStars Hire

INSTALL (2 minutes)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it (e.g. Documents\avastars-collector).
2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions and switch on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the avastars-collector folder.
4. Pin it: click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to AvaStars Collector.
5. Click the AvaStars icon. The side panel opens. Click Settings, enter:
     App address:      https://raas-drab.vercel.app   (your AvaStars Hire address)
     Inbound API key:  from AvaStars Hire > Settings > Keys & connections (Automation)
   and click "Save & test". It should say "Connected".

USE
1. Log in to Indeed / Naukri as usual and open the applicants list (or one candidate's profile).
2. In the side panel choose the job and the applied dates (Today, 7 days, or any range).
3. Click "Scan this page". For lists with several pages, open each page and scan again; results add up.
4. Untick anyone you don't want, then click "Send to AvaStars".
5. Optional: switch on "Resume catcher" - every CV you download from a portal is sent to AvaStars too.

It only reads pages you open and only when you click. Use it by hand, not as a non-stop bot.
